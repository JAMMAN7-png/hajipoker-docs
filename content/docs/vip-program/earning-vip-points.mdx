VIP points are the currency of HajiPoker's loyalty program. The more points you earn, the higher your [VIP tier](/docs/vip-program/vip-tiers-and-benefits), and the bigger your rakeback rewards. This guide explains how to earn points and climb the ladder efficiently.

## How You Earn Points

The primary way to earn VIP points on HajiPoker is by **playing poker**. Every time you play a hand at a cash game table and contribute to the rake, you earn VIP points. The more rake you generate, the more points you accumulate.

### Points from Cash Games

When you play poker at a cash game table:

1. A small rake is collected from each pot
2. Your contribution to the rake earns you VIP points
3. Points are added to your total automatically
4. Your VIP tier is recalculated in real time

### Points from Tournaments

Entering Sit & Go tournaments also contributes to your VIP points. The tournament fee portion of your buy-in counts toward your point earnings.

### Points from Challenges

Certain [VIP challenges](/docs/vip-program/challenges-and-rewards) award VIP points as part of their rewards, giving you an additional way to boost your total.

## Points Required for Each Tier

Here's a reminder of how many points you need for each tier:

| Tier | Points Required | Rakeback |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 0 | 5% |
| Silver | 1,000 | 10% |
| Gold | 5,000 | 15% |
| Platinum | 15,000 | 20% |
| Diamond | 50,000 | 30% |

## Tracking Your Points

You can always see your current VIP points in two places:

### Dashboard VIP Card
Your dashboard features a VIP progress card showing:
- Your current tier
- Total points earned
- Points needed for the next tier
- A visual progress bar

### Profile Page
Your profile displays your VIP tier badge, which is also visible to other players at the poker table.

## Tips to Earn Points Faster

### Play More Hands
The simplest way to earn more points is to play more poker. Every hand at a cash table that generates rake contributes to your VIP points. Players who put in more volume naturally climb the tiers faster.

### Play at Higher Stakes
Higher-stakes tables have larger pots, which means more rake is collected per hand. This translates directly into more VIP points per hand played. However, only play at stakes that are comfortable for your bankroll.

### Play Regularly
Consistency beats occasional big sessions. Playing a little every day keeps your points accumulating steadily. Set a routine that works for you.

### Complete VIP Challenges
Don't overlook the [challenges](/docs/vip-program/challenges-and-rewards)! Some challenges specifically award VIP points or chip bonuses that enhance your overall earning rate.

### Multi-Table
If you're an experienced player comfortable playing at multiple tables simultaneously, multi-tabling multiplies your hands per hour and accelerates point earning.

<Callout type="info">
Remember, the goal of the VIP program is to reward you for playing poker you'd enjoy anyway. Don't play at stakes you can't afford just to earn points faster — the best strategy is to play at comfortable stakes and let the points accumulate naturally.
</Callout>

## How Points Translate to Real Value

VIP points don't just unlock tiers — the tiers themselves provide ongoing value through rakeback. Here's what that looks like in practice:

**Example: A Gold tier player**
- Rakeback rate: 15%
- Monthly rake paid: 10,000 chips
- Monthly rakeback earned: **1,500 chips** (in bonus rewards)

**Example: A Diamond tier player**
- Rakeback rate: 30%
- Monthly rake paid: 10,000 chips
- Monthly rakeback earned: **3,000 chips** (in bonus rewards)

The difference is significant — a Diamond player earns double the rakeback of a Gold player on the same amount of play.

## Tier Up Notification

When you earn enough points to reach a new tier:

1. Your tier is upgraded **automatically** — no action needed from you
2. You'll see a **notification** congratulating you on your new tier
3. Your new, higher rakeback rate takes effect **immediately**
4. Your dashboard updates to reflect your new tier and badge

<Callout type="info">
Tier upgrades happen instantly. The moment your point total crosses the threshold for the next tier, your rakeback rate is upgraded right away.
</Callout>

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I lose VIP points?**
VIP points represent your total accumulated play history. Check the VIP terms on the website for details about point retention.

**Q: Do points carry over between seasons?**
Check the current VIP program terms for information about point resets or carryover.

**Q: Is there a limit to how many points I can earn?**
There's no cap on VIP point earnings. Play as much as you like and keep earning.

**Q: Do I earn points in free play or practice modes?**
VIP points are only earned from real-money gameplay where rake is collected.
