HajiPoker's **VIP Challenges** give you specific goals to work toward, with **bonus chip rewards** for completing them. They're a fun way to earn extra chips while playing the poker you already enjoy.

## What Are VIP Challenges?

VIP challenges are achievement-style goals that reward you with bonus chips when completed. They cover a range of activities — from playing a certain number of hands to reaching specific VIP tiers.

## Challenge Details

### 🃏 Hand Grinder — Play 100 Hands
**Reward: 100 chips**

The easiest challenge to get started with. Simply play 100 hands at any cash game table. Every hand counts, regardless of the game type or stake level. This is a great introduction to the challenge system.

**Tips:**
- Play at any stake level — low stakes work just fine
- Every hand counts, whether you fold preflop or play to showdown
- At a busy table, you can complete this in a single session

### 🏆 Tournament Champion — Win 10 Tournaments
**Reward: 500 chips**

This is the biggest challenge. Win 10 Sit & Go tournaments to claim 500 bonus chips. Only first-place finishes count — second and third place don't count toward this challenge.

**Tips:**
- Start with smaller tournaments to build your win count
- Heads-up tournaments (2 players) offer the best odds of winning any single tournament
- Review your [tournament strategy](/docs/tournaments/how-tournaments-work) to improve your win rate

### 👥 Social Butterfly — Refer 5 Friends
**Reward: 300 chips**

Invite 5 friends to HajiPoker using your personal referral code. When each friend signs up and starts playing, they count toward this challenge. See the [Referral Program](/docs/referrals/invite-friends) for details on how referrals work.

**Tips:**
- Share your referral link with poker-playing friends
- Your friends also earn 200 chips each for signing up with your code
- This challenge has a double benefit — you earn referral bonuses on top of the challenge reward

### 🥈 Silver Achiever — Reach Silver Tier
**Reward: 200 chips**

Climb to the Silver VIP tier by earning 1,000 VIP points. Once you reach Silver, you'll receive 200 bonus chips as a reward, on top of your upgraded 10% rakeback rate.

**Tips:**
- Play regularly at cash game tables to accumulate points
- Check your [VIP progress](/docs/vip-program/vip-tiers-and-benefits) on the dashboard to track your status
- This challenge rewards consistent play over time

### 🥇 Gold Achiever — Reach Gold Tier
**Reward: 500 chips**

Reach the Gold VIP tier by earning 5,000 VIP points. This is a significant milestone that rewards you with 500 bonus chips and a permanent upgrade to 15% rakeback.

**Tips:**
- Building on your Silver progress, keep playing consistently
- Higher-stakes tables generate more VIP points per hand
- This challenge takes dedicated play but the rakeback upgrade alone makes it worthwhile

<Callout type="info">
Challenge rewards are credited to your wallet as bonus chips the moment you complete the challenge. You'll see a notification and the chips will appear in your balance immediately.
</Callout>

## Tracking Your Challenge Progress

You can monitor your progress on all active challenges from your dashboard or profile page:

- Each challenge shows a **progress bar** or **counter** (e.g., "42/100 hands played")
- Completed challenges display a **completion badge**
- Rewards that have been claimed show a **claimed** status

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I complete the same challenge more than once?**
Each challenge can typically be completed once. Check the challenge details for any that may be repeatable.

**Q: Do challenge rewards expire?**
Once a challenge reward is credited to your wallet, the chips are yours to keep. They don't expire.

**Q: Do challenges have a time limit?**
Most challenges don't have a deadline — you can complete them at your own pace.

**Q: Where do I see my challenge progress?**
Your challenge progress is visible on your dashboard and profile page. Look for the challenges or achievements section.

<Callout type="warn">
Some challenges, like "Tournament Champion," require significant skill and time investment. Don't rush it — enjoy the journey and the chips will come naturally.
</Callout>
