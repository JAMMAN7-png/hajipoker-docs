HajiPoker rewards loyal players with a **VIP program** that gives you back a percentage of the rake you pay as bonus chips. The more you play, the higher your tier, and the more you earn back.

## The Five VIP Tiers

HajiPoker's VIP program has five tiers, each with increasing rewards:

| Tier | Badge | Min. Points Required | Rakeback Percentage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥉 **Bronze** | Bronze | 0 | **5%** |
| 🥈 **Silver** | Silver | 1,000 | **10%** |
| 🥇 **Gold** | Gold | 5,000 | **15%** |
| 💎 **Platinum** | Platinum | 15,000 | **20%** |
| 👑 **Diamond** | Diamond | 50,000 | **30%** |

Every player starts at the **Bronze** tier with **5% rakeback** from day one. As you accumulate VIP points, you'll automatically advance to higher tiers with better rewards.

## What Is Rakeback?

**Rakeback** is a reward where HajiPoker gives you back a percentage of the **rake** (service fee) collected from pots you play in.

Here's how it works:

1. You play poker hands at cash game tables
2. A small rake is collected from each pot
3. Based on your VIP tier, a percentage of that rake is returned to you as bonus chips
4. Rakeback is credited to your wallet automatically

**Example:**
- You're a **Gold** tier VIP (15% rakeback)
- Over a session, you contribute 1,000 chips in total rake
- You receive **150 chips** back as rakeback (15% × 1,000)

<Callout type="info">
Rakeback is essentially free money for playing poker. The more you play and the higher your tier, the more you earn back. At Diamond tier, you get 30% of all rake returned to you!
</Callout>

## Tier Benefits Breakdown

### Bronze (Starting Tier)
- **5% rakeback** on all cash game rake
- Access to all standard features
- VIP points tracking on your dashboard

### Silver (1,000 Points)
- **10% rakeback** — double the Bronze rate
- Everything from Bronze

### Gold (5,000 Points)
- **15% rakeback** — a significant boost in earnings
- Everything from Silver

### Platinum (15,000 Points)
- **20% rakeback** — one-fifth of all rake comes back to you
- Everything from Gold

### Diamond (50,000 Points)
- **30% rakeback** — the maximum reward, nearly one-third of all rake returned
- Everything from Platinum
- The highest status tier on HajiPoker

## Tier Progression

Your VIP tier is based on your total accumulated VIP points:

- **Bronze → Silver**: Earn 1,000 VIP points
- **Silver → Gold**: Earn 5,000 VIP points
- **Gold → Platinum**: Earn 15,000 VIP points
- **Platinum → Diamond**: Earn 50,000 VIP points

Your current tier and progress toward the next tier are displayed on the **VIP Progress** card on your dashboard.

## VIP Progress on Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows a **VIP Progress** card that includes:

- Your **current tier** and badge
- Your **total VIP points** earned
- A **progress bar** showing how close you are to the next tier
- Your **current rakeback percentage**

This gives you a clear view of where you stand and how much more you need to play to reach the next level.

## How to Reach Higher Tiers Faster

- **Play more hands** — The more hands you play, the more rake is generated, and the more VIP points you earn
- **Play at higher stakes** — Higher-stakes tables generate more rake per hand, which means more VIP points
- **Play regularly** — Consistent play adds up quickly over time
- **Complete challenges** — Some [VIP challenges](/docs/vip-program/challenges-and-rewards) award VIP points directly

<Callout type="warn">
VIP tier upgrades are automatic. As soon as you earn enough points to qualify for the next tier, you'll be promoted immediately — no need to apply or wait.
</Callout>

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I keep my tier forever?**
Your tier is based on your total accumulated VIP points. Check the VIP terms for details on tier maintenance.

**Q: Does rakeback apply to tournament fees?**
Rakeback applies to the rake collected from cash game pots. Tournament fees are separate.

**Q: How often is rakeback credited?**
Rakeback is calculated and credited to your wallet automatically on a regular basis.

**Q: Can I see how much rakeback I've earned?**
Yes, rakeback credits appear as "bonus" transactions in your [transaction history](/docs/wallet-and-payments/transaction-history).
