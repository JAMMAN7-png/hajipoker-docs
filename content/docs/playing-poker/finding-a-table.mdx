The **Lobby** is your gateway to the action on HajiPoker. It shows all available poker tables and gives you the tools to find exactly the kind of game you're looking for.

## Accessing the Lobby

To get to the lobby, click **Lobby** in the main navigation menu. You'll see a grid of **table cards**, each representing an active poker table that you can join.

## Reading a Table Card

Each table card in the lobby shows you the key information at a glance:

| Information | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| **Game type** | Whether it's Texas Hold'em, Omaha 4-Card, or Omaha 5-Card |
| **Stakes** | The small blind / big blind amounts (e.g., 25/50) |
| **Players** | How many players are currently seated versus the total seats available |
| **Quick Join** | A button to instantly sit down at this table |

## Filtering Tables

If the lobby has many tables, you can narrow down your options using the **filter options** at the top of the page:

### Filter by Game Type
Choose to see only:
- Texas Hold'em tables
- Omaha 4-Card tables
- Omaha 5-Card tables

### Filter by Stakes
Filter tables by their blind levels to match your budget. Lower-stakes tables are great for beginners or casual play, while higher-stakes tables attract more experienced players.

### Filter by Available Seats
If you're looking for a table with open seats, filter by the number of available spots. This helps you quickly find tables where you can jump right in without waiting.

## Table Sizes

HajiPoker tables support between **2 and 9 players**:

- **Heads-up** (2 players) — One-on-one poker for focused competition. See [Heads-Up](/docs/playing-poker/heads-up)
- **Short-handed** (3–6 players) — Faster action with fewer players
- **Full ring** (7–9 players) — Classic poker with a full table of opponents

## Blind Levels

Each table has a set **small blind** and **big blind** that determine the minimum bets:

- The **small blind** is the smaller forced bet posted by the player to the left of the dealer
- The **big blind** is the larger forced bet, typically double the small blind, posted by the next player

The default blind level is **25/50** (small blind of 25 chips, big blind of 50 chips), but tables are available at various stake levels.

<Callout type="info">
If you're new to HajiPoker, start with lower-stakes tables to get a feel for the platform. You can always move up to higher stakes as you build confidence and grow your chip stack.
</Callout>

## Quick Join

The fastest way to get into a game is the **Quick Join** button on each table card. Clicking it will immediately seat you at the table (assuming there's an open seat and you have enough chips for the minimum buy-in).

## Private Tables

If you don't see any private tables in the lobby, that's by design — private tables are password-protected and only visible to players who know the password. Learn more about [creating a private table](/docs/playing-poker/creating-a-private-table).

## Tips for Choosing a Table

1. **Match your bankroll** — A general rule is to have at least 20 times the big blind in your wallet when choosing a table. For a 25/50 table, that means at least 1,000 chips
2. **Check the player count** — Tables with fewer players mean you'll play more hands per hour
3. **Start small** — If you're trying a new game type like Omaha, start at lower stakes until you're comfortable
4. **Look at seat availability** — A full table might mean a better game, but you'll need to wait for a seat
