Found a table you like? Here's everything you need to know about joining a poker game on HajiPoker.

## Before You Join

Before you can sit down at a table, make sure you have:

- **Enough chips in your wallet** — Every table has a minimum buy-in amount. You need at least this many chips available in your wallet
- **A verified account** — Your email should be verified. We strongly recommend enabling [two-factor authentication](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication) too

## How to Join a Table

1. Browse the [Lobby](/docs/playing-poker/finding-a-table) and find a table you'd like to play at
2. Click the **Quick Join** button on the table card, or click the table to see more details first
3. A **buy-in dialog** will appear
4. Choose how many chips you want to bring to the table — this must be between the table's **minimum buy-in** and **maximum buy-in**
5. Click **Confirm** to take your seat
6. You're in! Wait for the next hand to begin and you'll be dealt in

## Understanding Buy-ins

Every poker table has two buy-in limits:

| Limit | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Minimum buy-in** | The fewest chips you can bring to the table |
| **Maximum buy-in** | The most chips you can bring to the table |

For example, a table might have a minimum buy-in of 1,000 chips and a maximum of 10,000 chips. You can bring any amount in that range.

<Callout type="info">
Your buy-in chips are transferred from your wallet to the table. When you leave the table, any remaining chips (plus winnings) are returned to your wallet.
</Callout>

## Choosing Your Buy-in Amount

How much should you buy in for? Here are some tips:

- **Buy in for the maximum** if you're confident and want the full strategic advantage — having more chips gives you more flexibility
- **Buy in for the minimum** if you want to test the waters at a new stake level or just want to play a few hands
- **A comfortable middle ground** is often the best approach — enough to play comfortably without risking too much

## Taking Your Seat

Once you buy in:

- You'll be assigned a seat at the table
- You'll see the other players, their chip stacks, and any ongoing hand
- If a hand is already in progress, you'll wait for the current hand to finish before being dealt in
- The **small blind** and **big blind** will rotate around the table naturally

## Table Rules at a Glance

- **Minimum players to start**: 2 — A hand won't begin until at least two players are seated and ready
- **Maximum players**: 9 — No more than nine players can sit at a single table
- **Blinds are automatic** — When it's your turn to post the small or big blind, the chips are automatically deducted from your stack

## Adding More Chips (Rebuying)

If your chip stack gets low during a session, you can add more chips from your wallet as long as:

- Your total stack doesn't exceed the table's maximum buy-in
- You have chips available in your wallet
- You're between hands (not during an active hand)

## Leaving a Table

When you're ready to stop playing:

1. Wait for the current hand to finish (you can't leave mid-hand)
2. Click the **Leave Table** or **Stand Up** button
3. Your remaining chips will be returned to your wallet immediately
4. Your final balance is reflected in your [transaction history](/docs/wallet-and-payments/transaction-history)

<Callout type="warn">
If you disconnect during a hand, your chips in the pot remain in play. Any unbet chips stay safe. The system will automatically fold your hand if you don't reconnect in time.
</Callout>
