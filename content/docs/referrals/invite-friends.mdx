HajiPoker's **referral program** rewards you for inviting friends to the platform. When a friend signs up using your referral code, **both of you** earn bonus chips. It's a win-win!

## Referral Rewards at a Glance

| Who | Reward |
|---|---|
| **You** (the referrer) | **500 chips** per friend who joins |
| **Your friend** (the referee) | **200 chips** welcome bonus |

That's right — you earn **500 chips** for every friend you bring to HajiPoker, and your friend gets **200 chips** to start playing with. There's no better way to start than with free chips!

## How the Referral Program Works

### Step 1: Find Your Referral Code

Every HajiPoker account has a unique referral code. To find yours:

1. Go to your **Profile** or **Settings** page
2. Look for the **Referrals** section
3. You'll see your **personal referral code** — a unique code assigned to your account
4. You'll also see a **referral link** that you can share directly

### Step 2: Share Your Code or Link

Share your referral code or link with friends who might enjoy playing poker:

- Send it via text message or chat
- Post it on social media
- Share it in your poker group chats
- Include it in an email to poker-playing friends

### Step 3: Your Friend Signs Up

When your friend creates a HajiPoker account:

1. They click your referral link, or
2. They enter your referral code during registration
3. Their account is linked to yours as a referral
4. The referral status is set to **pending** until they start playing

### Step 4: Both of You Get Rewarded

Once your friend's referral is confirmed:

- **Your friend** receives **200 chips** as a welcome bonus
- **You** receive **500 chips** as a referral reward
- The referral status changes from **pending** → **active** → **rewarded**

The bonus chips are automatically added to both wallets.

<Callout type="info">
Your friend needs to sign up and start playing for the referral to be confirmed. Simply creating an account may not be enough — they need to be active on the platform.
</Callout>

## Referral Statuses

Each referral goes through three stages:

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Your friend has signed up but hasn't started playing yet |
| **Active** | Your friend has started playing on the platform |
| **Rewarded** | Both you and your friend have received your bonus chips |

You can track the status of all your referrals on the referrals page in your profile.

## Tracking Your Referrals

The **Referrals** section in your profile or settings shows:

- How many friends you've referred
- The status of each referral (pending, active, or rewarded)
- Your total earnings from referrals
- Your unique referral code and shareable link

## Tips for Successful Referrals

1. **Share with poker players** — People who already enjoy poker are the most likely to sign up and play
2. **Mention the free chips** — Let your friends know they'll get 200 free chips just for joining with your code
3. **Play together** — [Create a private table](/docs/playing-poker/creating-a-private-table) and invite your friends for a game. It's more fun to play with people you know!
4. **Share across channels** — Post your link on social media, poker forums, and messaging groups for maximum reach
5. **Time it right** — If HajiPoker is running any promotions, share your code then for extra appeal

That means for 5 successful referrals, you could earn:

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| 5 × referral bonus (500 each) | 2,500 chips |
| Social Butterfly challenge reward | 300 chips |

Plus your 5 friends each get 200 chips, for a combined **1,000 chips** among your friends.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?**
There's no limit! Refer as many friends as you like and earn 500 chips for each one.

**Q: What if my friend forgets to use my referral code?**
Unfortunately, the referral code must be used during registration. If your friend signed up without it, the referral can't be applied retroactively. Make sure they use your code or link when creating their account.

**Q: Can I refer someone who already has an account?**
No. Referral codes only work for new accounts. If someone already has a HajiPoker account, they can't be linked as your referral.

**Q: When do I receive my referral bonus?**
Your bonus is credited once the referral reaches the "rewarded" status — meaning your friend has signed up and started playing.

**Q: Can I use my own referral code to create a second account?**
No. Creating multiple accounts is against HajiPoker's terms of service. Referral codes are meant for different people.
