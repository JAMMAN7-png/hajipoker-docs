title: Signing In to Your Account
description: All the ways to sign in — email, Google, Discord, Telegram, or Passkey
---

There are several ways to sign in to your HajiPoker account. Choose the method that works best for you.

## Sign In with Email and Password

1. Click the **Sign In** button on the top-right corner of the website
2. Enter your **email address** and **password**
3. Click **Sign In**
4. If you have Two-Factor Authentication enabled, you'll be asked to enter your 6-digit code (see [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication) for details)

## Sign In with Google

1. Click **Sign In** on the website
2. Click the **Continue with Google** button
3. Select your Google account or sign in to Google if prompted
4. You'll be redirected back to HajiPoker and signed in automatically

## Sign In with Discord

1. Click **Sign In** on the website
2. Click the **Continue with Discord** button
3. Authorize HajiPoker in Discord if this is your first time
4. You'll be redirected back and signed in

## Sign In with Telegram

1. Click **Sign In** on the website
2. Click the **Continue with Telegram** button
3. Confirm the login in your Telegram app
4. You'll be signed in automatically

## Sign In with a Passkey

If you've set up a Passkey (fingerprint, Face ID, or security key), you can use it for a password-free sign in:

1. Click **Sign In** on the website
2. Click **Sign in with Passkey**
3. Follow the prompt on your device — use your fingerprint, face scan, or security key
4. You're in! No password needed

## Staying Signed In

Your session lasts for **7 days**. After that, you'll need to sign in again. If you use HajiPoker regularly, your session refreshes automatically — so you'll stay signed in as long as you visit at least once a week.

## Trouble Signing In?

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Forgot your password | Click **Forgot Password** on the sign-in page — we'll send you a reset link |
| Account locked after too many attempts | Wait a few minutes and try again |
| Social login not working | Make sure pop-ups are allowed for HajiPoker in your browser |
| "Email not verified" error | Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link |
| 2FA code not accepted | Make sure the time on your phone is correct (TOTP codes are time-based) |
