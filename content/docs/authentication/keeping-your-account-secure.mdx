title: Keeping Your Account Secure
description: Best practices for protecting your HajiPoker account
---

Your HajiPoker account is connected to your wallet and personal information. Here are the best practices to keep it safe.

## Security Checklist

- Use a **strong, unique password** (score of Fair or higher on the strength meter)
- **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** — this is the single most important thing you can do
- **Save your 2FA backup codes** in a secure location
- **Verify your email address** after signing up
- Consider setting up a **passkey** for phishing-resistant sign-in
- Never share your password or 2FA codes with anyone

## Password Best Practices

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use 12+ characters with mixed types | Use your username or email as your password |
| Use a unique password for HajiPoker | Reuse passwords from other websites |
| Use a password manager | Write passwords on sticky notes |
| Change your password if you suspect it was compromised | Share your password with anyone — HajiPoker staff will never ask for it |

## Recognizing Phishing Attempts

HajiPoker will **never**:
- Ask for your password via email, chat, or at the poker table
- Send you links to sign in from unexpected emails
- Ask you to send cryptocurrency or chips to "verify" your account

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from HajiPoker:
1. **Do not click** any links in the message
2. **Do not share** any personal information
3. Report it to our support team

## Signing Out

Always sign out when using a shared or public computer:

1. Click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner
2. Click **Sign Out**

Your session automatically expires after **7 days** of inactivity, but it's always safer to sign out manually on shared devices.

## What to Do If Your Account Is Compromised

If you suspect unauthorized access to your account:

1. **Change your password immediately** from Account Settings
2. **Enable 2FA** if it's not already active
3. **Review your recent activity** for any unfamiliar actions
4. **Contact our support team** and let us know what happened
5. **Check your email** for any password reset requests you didn't make
