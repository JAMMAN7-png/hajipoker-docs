Welcome to HajiPoker! Creating an account takes less than a minute and gets you access to all our poker tables, tournaments, and features.

## How to Sign Up

1. Click the **Sign Up** button on the top-right corner of the website
2. Fill in the following details:
   - **Email address** — This will be your primary login and where we send important notifications
   - **Username** — Your public display name at the poker tables. Choose something you like — other players will see this!
   - **Password** — Must be at least 8 characters long. We recommend using a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
3. Click **Create Account**
4. Check your email inbox for a **verification email** and click the link to confirm your account
5. You're in! You'll be signed in automatically after verifying your email

## Password Tips

When you create your password, you'll see a **strength indicator** that rates it from Very Weak to Very Strong:

| Strength | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Very Weak | Too easy to guess — please choose a stronger one |
| Weak | Offers minimal protection — try adding more variety |
| Fair | Acceptable, but could be stronger |
| Strong | Good choice! |
| Very Strong | Excellent — your account is well protected |

We recommend aiming for at least **Fair** strength. Avoid using your name, email, or common words like "password123".

## Quick Sign Up with Social Accounts

Don't want to create a new password? You can sign up instantly using:

- **Google** — Use your existing Google account
- **Discord** — Great if you're already in our Discord community
- **Telegram** — Connect with your Telegram account

Just click the corresponding button on the sign-up page and authorize HajiPoker. We'll create your account automatically using your social profile information.

## What Happens After Sign Up

- A **wallet** is automatically created for your account — you'll use this to manage your chips and balances
- Your username is generated from your display name if you signed up via a social account
- You can start browsing the lobby and joining tables right away

## Didn't Receive the Verification Email?

- Check your **spam** or **junk** folder
- Make sure you entered your email address correctly
- Wait a few minutes — sometimes emails take a moment to arrive
- If you still don't see it, try signing in and requesting a new verification email from your account settings
