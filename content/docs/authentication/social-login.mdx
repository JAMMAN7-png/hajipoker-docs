HajiPoker lets you sign in using your existing social accounts — no need to remember another password.

## Supported Social Accounts

| Provider | How It Works |
|---|---|
| **Google** | Use your Gmail or Google Workspace account |
| **Discord** | Use your Discord account — perfect if you're in our community server |
| **Telegram** | Use your Telegram account for quick mobile-friendly login |

## Signing Up with a Social Account

If you don't have a HajiPoker account yet:

1. Click **Sign Up** on the website
2. Click the social provider button (e.g., **Continue with Google**)
3. Authorize HajiPoker to access your basic profile information
4. Your account is created automatically — a username is generated from your profile name
5. A wallet is created for you right away
6. You're signed in and ready to play!

<Callout type="info">
**What information do we access?** Only your name, email address, and profile picture. We never post on your behalf or access any other data.
</Callout>

## Signing In with a Social Account

If you already have an account:

1. Click **Sign In**
2. Click the social provider you used when signing up
3. Confirm the authorization if prompted
4. You'll be signed in immediately

## Using Multiple Social Accounts

Your HajiPoker account is tied to the email address from your social provider. If your Google and Discord accounts use the **same email address**, they'll both connect to the same HajiPoker account.

If they use **different email addresses**, they'll create separate accounts.

## Google Sign-In

1. Click **Continue with Google**
2. Select your Google account from the list (or sign in to Google)
3. Google will ask you to confirm sharing your name and email with HajiPoker
4. Click **Allow**
5. Done — you're signed in

## Discord Sign-In

1. Click **Continue with Discord**
2. If you're already logged in to Discord in your browser, you'll see an authorization screen
3. Click **Authorize**
4. If you're not logged in to Discord, enter your Discord credentials first
5. Done — you're signed in

## Telegram Sign-In

1. Click **Continue with Telegram**
2. A Telegram authorization widget will appear
3. Confirm the login in your Telegram app (you may receive a notification)
4. Done — you're signed in

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I add a password later?**
Yes! Even if you signed up with a social account, you can add a password from your Account Settings. This gives you the option to sign in with either method.

**Is social login secure?**
Yes. We use the industry-standard OAuth 2.0 protocol. HajiPoker never sees or stores your social account password — authentication happens directly with Google, Discord, or Telegram.
