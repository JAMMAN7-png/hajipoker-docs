---
title: Resetting Your Password
description: How to reset your HajiPoker password if you forgot it
---

Forgot your password? No worries — you can reset it in just a few steps.

## How to Reset Your Password

1. Go to the **Sign In** page
2. Click **Forgot Password?** below the password field
3. Enter the **email address** associated with your HajiPoker account
4. Click **Send Reset Link**
5. Check your email inbox — you'll receive a message with a **password reset link**
6. Click the link in the email
7. Enter your **new password** and confirm it
8. Click **Reset Password**
9. You'll be signed in automatically with your new password

## Choosing a Strong New Password

- Must be at least **8 characters** long
- Use a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
- Avoid using your username, email, or common words
- The password strength indicator will help guide you — aim for **Fair** or higher

## Didn't Receive the Reset Email?

- Check your **spam** or **junk** folder
- Make sure you entered the correct email address — it must match the one you signed up with
- Wait 2-3 minutes for the email to arrive
- If you signed up with Google, Discord, or Telegram only, you may not have a password set. Try signing in with your social account instead

## The Reset Link Isn't Working

- Reset links expire after a short time for security reasons. If your link has expired, go back to the **Forgot Password** page and request a new one
- Make sure you're clicking the most recent reset link if you requested multiple times

## Signed In with a Social Account?

If you originally created your account using **Google**, **Discord**, or **Telegram**, you may not have a password at all. In that case:

- Simply sign in using the same social account you used to sign up
- You don't need a password — your social account handles authentication for you

<Callout type="info">
You can always add a password to your account later from your account settings, even if you signed up with a social account.
</Callout>
