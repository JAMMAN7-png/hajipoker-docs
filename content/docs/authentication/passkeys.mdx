Passkeys let you sign in to HajiPoker using your fingerprint, face scan, or a hardware security key — no password required.

## What Is a Passkey?

A passkey is a modern, phishing-resistant way to sign in. Instead of typing a password, you use something built into your device:

- **Fingerprint** (Touch ID on Mac, fingerprint sensor on Android/Windows)
- **Face scan** (Face ID on iPhone/iPad)
- **Security key** (YubiKey, Titan Key, etc.)
- **Screen lock** (PIN or pattern on your phone)

Passkeys are stored securely on your device and synced across your Apple, Google, or Microsoft ecosystem. They are considered **more secure than passwords** because they can't be phished, guessed, or leaked.

## How to Set Up a Passkey

1. Sign in to your HajiPoker account (using your password or social login)
2. Go to **Account Settings** then **Security**
3. Click **Add Passkey**
4. Your browser will prompt you to create a passkey
5. Use your fingerprint, face scan, or security key as instructed
6. Give your passkey a name (e.g., "MacBook Pro" or "iPhone")
7. Click **Save** — your passkey is now registered

You can add multiple passkeys for different devices.

## Signing In with a Passkey

1. Go to the **Sign In** page
2. Click **Sign in with Passkey**
3. Your browser will prompt you to use your fingerprint, face scan, or security key
4. Authenticate with your device
5. You're signed in — no password needed!

## Which Devices Support Passkeys?

| Device | Support |
|---|---|
| **iPhone / iPad** | iOS 16+ with Face ID or Touch ID |
| **Mac** | macOS Ventura+ with Touch ID or iPhone proximity |
| **Android** | Android 9+ with screen lock |
| **Windows** | Windows 10+ with Windows Hello |
| **Chrome / Edge / Safari / Firefox** | All major browsers support passkeys |

## Managing Your Passkeys

- Go to **Account Settings** then **Security** to see all registered passkeys
- You can **remove** a passkey at any time
- We recommend having at least one backup sign-in method (password or social login) in case you lose access to all your passkey devices

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do passkeys replace my password?**
Passkeys are an alternative to your password, not a replacement. You can keep both and use whichever is more convenient.

**What if I lose my device?**
If your passkeys are synced via iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, or similar, they'll be available on your other devices. If not, sign in with your password or social account and remove the lost device's passkey.

**Are passkeys secure?**
Yes — passkeys use public-key cryptography and are considered the gold standard for authentication security. They cannot be phished, and the private key never leaves your device.
