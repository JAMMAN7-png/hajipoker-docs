One of the most exciting parts of tournament poker is the prize pool. This guide explains exactly how prizes are calculated and paid out in HajiPoker Sit & Go tournaments.

## How the Prize Pool Is Created

The prize pool for a Sit & Go tournament is made up of all the buy-ins from registered players. The fee (service charge) is not included in the prize pool — it goes to HajiPoker for running the tournament.

**Example:**
- Tournament buy-in: 1,000 chips + 100 chip fee
- 8 players register
- **Prize pool** = 8 × 1,000 = **8,000 chips**
- Total collected: 8 × 1,100 = 8,800 chips (800 in fees)

## Payout Structure

How the prize pool is divided depends on how many players entered the tournament. HajiPoker uses two payout structures:

### Tournaments with 7 or More Players

When 7 or more players enter, the **top 3 finishers** are paid:

| Position | Prize Share |
|---|---|
| 🥇 **1st Place** | **50%** of the prize pool |
| 🥈 **2nd Place** | **30%** of the prize pool |
| 🥉 **3rd Place** | **20%** of the prize pool |

**Example with 8 players and 8,000 chip prize pool:**

| Position | Share | Prize |
|---|---|---|
| 1st Place | 50% | 4,000 chips |
| 2nd Place | 30% | 2,400 chips |
| 3rd Place | 20% | 1,600 chips |

### Tournaments with 2 to 6 Players

When 2 to 6 players enter, the **top 2 finishers** are paid:

| Position | Prize Share |
|---|---|
| 🥇 **1st Place** | **65%** of the prize pool |
| 🥈 **2nd Place** | **35%** of the prize pool |

**Example with 4 players and 4,000 chip prize pool:**

| Position | Share | Prize |
|---|---|---|
| 1st Place | 65% | 2,600 chips |
| 2nd Place | 35% | 1,400 chips |

<Callout type="info">
Payouts are calculated automatically. When you finish in a paying position, the chips are instantly sent to your wallet — no waiting required.
</Callout>

## The Money Bubble

The **"money bubble"** is the critical point in a tournament where one more elimination separates the remaining players from the paying positions.

**Example in an 8-player tournament:**
- Top 3 get paid
- When 4 players remain, the next player eliminated finishes 4th (no prize)
- The 4th place finisher is "the bubble boy"

This is one of the most intense moments in tournament poker. Players on the bubble often play very cautiously to avoid being eliminated just before the money, while aggressive players try to take advantage of this caution.

## How Prizes Are Delivered

When you finish in a paying position:

1. The tournament ends (or you are eliminated in a paying position)
2. Your finishing position is confirmed
3. Your prize chips are **automatically** added to your wallet
4. A transaction record appears in your [transaction history](/docs/wallet-and-payments/transaction-history)
5. You receive a notification confirming your prize

There is no delay — prizes are instant.

## Strategy Around Prize Payouts

Understanding the payout structure affects your strategy:

- **In small tournaments (2–6 players):** Only 2 places pay, so the jump from 3rd (nothing) to 2nd (35%) is huge. Play to survive into the top 2, then play to win
- **In larger tournaments (7+ players):** Three places pay, giving you a wider safety net. However, the jump from 3rd (20%) to 1st (50%) is significant — always play to win, not just to cash
- **Near the bubble:** Consider whether folding to secure a min-cash is worth it, or if you should push for a higher finish
- **Heads-up at the end:** The prize difference between 1st and 2nd is always significant. This is where aggressive play is most rewarded

<Callout type="warn">
Remember: finishing 1st pays significantly more than 2nd or 3rd. Don't play just to "make the money" — play to win the tournament.
</Callout>

## Common Questions

**Q: What happens if the tournament doesn't fill up?**
Sit & Go tournaments start with a minimum of 2 players. Even a small tournament will have a prize pool based on the registered buy-ins.

**Q: Are the payouts guaranteed?**
The prize pool is made up of actual buy-ins, so it's always fully funded. There are no guaranteed prize pools — the pool equals the sum of all buy-ins.

**Q: Can I see the payout structure before joining?**
Yes, the payout structure is displayed in the tournament details before you register.
