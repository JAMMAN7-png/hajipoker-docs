Joining a Sit & Go tournament on HajiPoker is quick and simple. This guide walks you through the entire process, from finding a tournament to taking your seat.

## Finding Tournaments

To browse available tournaments:

1. Click **Tournaments** in the main navigation menu
2. You'll see a list of available Sit & Go tournaments
3. Each tournament listing shows:
   - The buy-in amount (plus fee)
   - The game type (Texas Hold'em, Omaha 4-Card, or Omaha 5-Card)
   - How many players have registered
   - How many players are needed to start

## How to Register

1. Find a tournament you'd like to play in
2. Click on it to see the full details
3. Review the buy-in amount and fee
4. Make sure you have enough chips in your [wallet](/docs/wallet-and-payments/understanding-your-wallet)
5. Click **Register** or **Join Tournament**
6. The buy-in plus fee will be deducted from your wallet
7. You're registered! Wait for the tournament to fill up and start

<Callout type="info">
Sit & Go tournaments start as soon as the minimum number of players (2) have registered. Larger tournaments may wait for more players. You'll be notified when the tournament begins.
</Callout>

## Before the Tournament Starts

After registering, you'll be in a waiting state:

- A **countdown** or **waiting screen** shows how many more players are needed
- You'll receive a **notification** when the tournament is about to begin
- Stay on the site — when the tournament starts, you'll be automatically seated at the table
- All players start with **10,000 chips** regardless of the buy-in amount

## During the Tournament

Once the tournament begins:

### Blinds
- Blinds start at **10/20** and increase every **5 minutes**
- See the [full blind schedule](/docs/tournaments/how-tournaments-work) for all 10 levels

### Gameplay
- Play follows the normal poker rules for the selected game type
- The same [actions](/docs/playing-poker/poker-actions) are available: fold, check, call, bet, raise, and all-in

### Eliminations
- Players are eliminated when they lose all their chips
- You cannot rebuy or add chips — what you start with is what you have
- The tournament continues until one player has all the chips

### Position Tracking
- You can see your current position and chip count relative to other players
- A leaderboard or player list shows who's still in and their stack sizes

## When You're Eliminated

If you lose all your chips:

- You'll see your **final position** (e.g., "You finished 4th out of 8 players")
- If you finished **in the money** (a paying position), your prize is automatically sent to your wallet
- You can return to the lobby to join another game or tournament

<Callout type="warn">
Tournament chips have no direct cash value. Even if you have 50,000 tournament chips, your prize is determined by your finishing position, not your chip count. Coming in 1st with 10,001 chips pays the same as coming in 1st with 90,000 chips.
</Callout>

## Tips for Tournament Success

1. **Be patient early** — Blinds are low at the start. Don't risk your entire stack on marginal hands
2. **Watch the blind clock** — Know when blinds are about to increase and plan accordingly
3. **Protect your stack** — Avoid unnecessary risks when you have a comfortable stack
4. **Push when short** — If your stack drops below 10 big blinds, look for opportunities to go all-in with decent hands
5. **Adjust to the table** — As players are eliminated, the table gets shorter. Adjust your strategy — play more hands at a short-handed table
6. **Know the payout structure** — Understand how many places pay and adjust your risk-taking near the money bubble. See [Prizes and Payouts](/docs/tournaments/prizes-and-payouts)

## Can I Unregister?

If the tournament hasn't started yet and you change your mind, you may be able to unregister and receive a refund of your buy-in and fee. Check the tournament details for cancellation options.

## Common Questions

**Q: What game types are available in tournaments?**
Sit & Go tournaments are available in Texas Hold'em, Omaha 4-Card, and Omaha 5-Card.

**Q: Can I play in multiple tournaments at once?**
Check the tournament interface for details on multi-tabling in tournaments.

**Q: What happens if I disconnect during a tournament?**
If you disconnect, your hands will be automatically folded. Your chips remain in play, and if you reconnect, you can continue playing from your current position. However, you may lose chips to blinds while disconnected.

**Q: How long do tournaments take?**
A Sit & Go tournament typically lasts 30 minutes to an hour, depending on the number of players and how the action unfolds. The 10-level blind structure ensures the tournament reaches a conclusion.
