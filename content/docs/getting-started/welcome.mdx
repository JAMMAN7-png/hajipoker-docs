Welcome to **HajiPoker** — the online poker platform where you can play Texas Hold'em, Omaha, and tournament poker from anywhere in the world. Whether you're a seasoned poker pro or picking up the game for the first time, this help center will walk you through everything you need to know.

## What is HajiPoker?

HajiPoker is a modern online poker platform that offers:

- **Multiple game types** — Play Texas Hold'em, 4-Card Omaha, or 5-Card Omaha
- **Cash games** — Join tables with real chip stakes at any time
- **Sit & Go tournaments** — Compete against other players for big prize pools
- **Heads-up play** — Challenge a single opponent one-on-one
- **Provably fair dealing** — Every hand can be independently verified for fairness
- **VIP rewards** — Earn points as you play and unlock rakeback bonuses up to 30%
- **Crypto & card payments** — Deposit using 25+ cryptocurrencies or traditional payment methods

## Quick Start Guide

Here's the fastest way to start playing:

1. **Create your account** — [Sign up](/docs/authentication/creating-your-account) with your email, Google, Discord, or Telegram
2. **Secure your account** — Set up [two-factor authentication](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication) for extra protection
3. **Add funds** — [Deposit chips](/docs/wallet-and-payments/depositing-funds) using crypto or a credit card
4. **Find a table** — Browse the [lobby](/docs/playing-poker/finding-a-table) and pick a game that suits your budget
5. **Play!** — [Join a table](/docs/playing-poker/joining-a-game) and enjoy the game
