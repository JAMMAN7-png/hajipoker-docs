When you sign in to HajiPoker, you'll land on your **Dashboard** — your home base for everything. This guide walks you through the main areas of the website so you can find what you need quickly.

## The Dashboard

Your dashboard gives you a snapshot of your account at a glance. Here's what you'll see:

### Balance Card
Your current chip balance is displayed prominently at the top. This shows how many chips you have available to play with. From here, you can quickly navigate to the deposit or withdrawal pages.

### Active Tables
If you're currently seated at any poker tables, they'll be listed here. Click on any active table to jump right back into the action.

### Recent Hands
A quick view of your most recently played hands, showing the game type, result, and how many chips you won or lost.

### VIP Progress
Your current VIP tier and a progress bar showing how close you are to reaching the next level. You'll also see your current rakeback percentage.

### Notifications
Important updates like tournament starting alerts, deposit confirmations, and promotional offers appear here.

### System Status
A small indicator showing whether all HajiPoker services are running normally. Green means everything is working perfectly.

## Main Navigation

The main menu gives you quick access to all areas of HajiPoker:

| Menu Item | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Returns you to your home screen overview |
| **Lobby** | Browse all available poker tables and join a game |
| **Tournaments** | View upcoming and active Sit & Go tournaments |
| **Store** | Browse the chip store for special offers |
| **Gifts** | Send or receive chip gifts from other players |
| **Profile** | View and edit your player profile |
| **Settings** | Manage your account preferences and security |

## The Lobby

The **Lobby** is where you find poker games to join. It displays a list of active tables as cards, each showing:

- **Game type** — Texas Hold'em, Omaha 4-Card, or Omaha 5-Card
- **Stakes** — The small blind and big blind amounts
- **Players** — How many seats are taken versus available
- **Quick Join** button — Instantly sit down at the table

You can filter tables by game type, stake level, or number of available seats to find exactly the kind of game you're looking for.

<Callout type="info">
Use the filter options at the top of the lobby to narrow down tables. You can filter by game type, stakes, and how many seats are open.
</Callout>

## Your Profile

Your **Profile** page shows your public information that other players can see:

- Your username and avatar
- Your VIP tier badge
- Your playing statistics
- Your recent activity

You can customize your profile from the **Settings** page.

## Settings

The **Settings** page lets you manage:

- **Account details** — Update your email, username, or password
- **Security** — Enable two-factor authentication, manage passkeys
- **Preferences** — Customize your playing experience

## Other Pages

HajiPoker also has several informational pages accessible from the footer of the website:

- **About** — Learn more about HajiPoker and our mission
- **Contact** — Alternative ways to reach our support team
- **FAQ** — Quick answers to the most common questions
- **Privacy Policy** — How we handle and protect your data
- **Terms of Service** — The rules and agreements for using HajiPoker

## Social Features

HajiPoker includes social features that let you connect with other players:

- **Friends list** — Add other players as friends
- **Gifts** — Send chip gifts to friends as a gesture of goodwill
- **Referrals** — [Invite friends](/docs/referrals/invite-friends) and both of you earn bonus chips

## Quick Tips for New Players

- **Bookmark the lobby** — It's the fastest way to jump into a game
- **Check your dashboard regularly** — Keep an eye on your balance and VIP progress
- **Set up 2FA first** — Protect your account before depositing funds. See [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication)
- **Start with lower stakes** — Get comfortable with the platform before playing at higher-stakes tables
- **Use live chat** — If you're ever confused, our support team is just a click away in the bottom-right corner
