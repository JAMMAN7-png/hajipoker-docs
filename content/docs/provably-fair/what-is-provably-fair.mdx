**Provably fair** is a system that lets you independently verify that every poker hand on HajiPoker was dealt randomly and fairly — without having to take our word for it. It's the gold standard for transparency in online gaming.

## Why Does It Matter?

One of the biggest concerns players have with online poker is trust: "How do I know the cards aren't rigged?" Provably fair technology solves this problem completely. It provides mathematical proof that:

- Cards are dealt randomly
- No one — not even HajiPoker — can manipulate the outcome
- You can verify any hand after it's played

## How It Works (4 Simple Steps)

HajiPoker's provably fair system works in four steps:

### Step 1: The Server Creates an Encrypted Seed 🔒

Before any cards are dealt, the server generates a **secret seed** — a random string of characters. This seed is then **encrypted** (scrambled) so nobody can read it. The encrypted version is shared with you at the start of the hand.

Think of it like putting a prediction in a sealed envelope before the game starts. You can see the envelope, but you can't open it until the hand is over.

### Step 2: Random Cards Are Generated 🎲

The seed is used to generate the card order for the hand. Because the seed is random and was locked in before any player acted, the cards are guaranteed to be random and unbiased.

The mathematical process ensures that:
- Every card has an equal chance of being dealt
- The order cannot be predicted or influenced
- The result is determined before any player makes a decision

### Step 3: Cards Are Dealt to Players 🃏

The cards are dealt according to the randomly generated order. During the hand, everything plays out normally — you see your hole cards, the community cards are revealed in stages (flop, turn, river), and players make their betting decisions.

### Step 4: Verification After the Hand ✅

After the hand is complete, the **original seed is revealed** (the envelope is opened). You can now:

- See the original seed that determined the card order
- Run the same mathematical process yourself to confirm the cards match
- Verify that the seed was created *before* the hand started (proving it wasn't changed)

This proves beyond any doubt that the cards were dealt fairly.

## What This Means for You

- **Trust through transparency** — You never have to wonder if the game is fair. You can check any hand, any time
- **No manipulation possible** — Because the seed is locked before the hand, nobody can change the outcome — not even HajiPoker staff
- **Complete history** — Your recent hands are stored so you can go back and verify any of them
- **Peace of mind** — Play knowing that the deck is genuinely random and tamper-proof

## How Is This Different from Regular Online Poker?

At many traditional online poker sites, you're asked to simply trust that the software is fair. They may have certifications or audits, but you can't personally verify individual hands.

With provably fair technology on HajiPoker, you don't need to trust anyone:

| Traditional Online Poker | HajiPoker (Provably Fair) |
|---|---|
| "Trust us, it's fair" | "Verify it yourself" |
| Periodic third-party audits | Every single hand is verifiable |
| No way to check individual hands | Built-in hand verifier |
| Requires trust | Requires only math |

## Is the System Really Tamper-Proof?

Yes. The key is that the seed is **encrypted and committed before the hand starts**. This means:

- HajiPoker cannot change the seed after seeing player actions
- The encryption ensures no one can read the seed during the hand
- After the hand, anyone can verify the seed matches the encrypted version
- The math is standard and can be independently verified
