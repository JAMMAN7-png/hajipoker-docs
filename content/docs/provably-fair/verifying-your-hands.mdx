HajiPoker makes it easy to verify that any hand you've played was dealt fairly. This guide shows you exactly how to check your hands using the built-in verification tools.

## Where to Find Your Hand History

Your recently played hands are available in several places:

- **Dashboard** — The "Recent Hands" section shows your most recent games
- **Hand History** — A detailed log of all hands you've played
- **At the table** — After each hand, you can view the hand details

## How to Verify a Hand

### Step 1: Find the Hand

Navigate to your hand history and find the specific hand you want to verify. Each hand shows:

- The hand number
- The game type (Texas Hold'em, Omaha 4-Card, or Omaha 5-Card)
- The cards that were dealt
- The outcome (who won and how much)
- The date and time

### Step 2: Open the Verification Tool

Click on the hand to view its details. You'll see a **Verify** button or a **Provably Fair** section that shows the verification information.

### Step 3: View the Seed Information

The hand verifier displays:

- **Encrypted seed** — The locked version that was committed before the hand started
- **Revealed seed** — The original seed, now visible after the hand is complete
- **Cards dealt** — The full sequence of cards generated from the seed

### Step 4: Confirm the Match

The verification tool automatically:

1. Takes the revealed seed
2. Runs the same card-generation process
3. Compares the result with the cards that were actually dealt
4. Shows you a **verification result** — confirmed fair or flagged

If the cards generated from the seed match exactly what was dealt during the hand, the verification passes. This proves the hand was fair.

<Callout type="info">
The verification process is automatic — you don't need to do any calculations yourself. Just click "Verify" and the tool does the rest.
</Callout>

## Understanding the Verification Display

### The Seed Display

When viewing a verified hand, you'll see two important pieces of information:

- **Encrypted Seed**: This is the scrambled version that was created and shared before the hand. It proves the outcome was determined in advance
- **Server Seed**: This is the original seed revealed after the hand. When encrypted, it should match the encrypted seed exactly

### The Verification Status

The hand verifier will show one of these results:

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Verified** | The cards match the seed perfectly — the hand was provably fair |
| ❌ **Failed** | Something doesn't match — this should never happen. Contact [support](/docs/support/getting-help) immediately |

## Browsing Recent Hands

You can browse through all your recent hands and verify any of them:

1. Go to your hand history
2. Scroll through your recent hands
3. Click on any hand to expand the details
4. Use the verification feature to check the fairness

The history keeps a record of all your hands, so you can go back and verify hands from days or weeks ago.

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I have to verify every hand?**
No! Verification is completely optional. It's there for your peace of mind. Many players never verify a single hand, and that's perfectly fine. The system is provably fair regardless of whether you check.

**Q: Can I verify hands from a long time ago?**
Yes. Your hand history stores the seed and card information for past hands. You can go back and verify any hand at any time.

**Q: What if a verification fails?**
This should not happen under normal circumstances. If you encounter a verification failure, contact our [support team](/docs/support/getting-help) immediately with the hand details. We take fairness extremely seriously.

**Q: Can someone else verify my hands?**
The seed information is associated with the hand itself. Anyone who has access to the seed data can independently run the verification.

**Q: Does verification slow down the game?**
Not at all. Verification happens after the hand is complete and is entirely separate from gameplay. You can verify hands at your leisure without affecting ongoing games.

**Q: How is this different from a regular hand history?**
A regular hand history just shows you what happened. The provably fair verification proves *why* it happened — by showing you the mathematical seed that determined the cards, you can confirm the dealing was genuinely random.

<Callout type="warn">
If you ever suspect an issue with the fairness of a hand, don't hesitate to verify it using the built-in tool. If the verification confirms it's fair, you can play with confidence. If something seems off, contact support right away.
</Callout>
