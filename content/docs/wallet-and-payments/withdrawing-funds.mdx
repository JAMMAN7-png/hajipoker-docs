When you're ready to cash out your winnings, HajiPoker makes it easy to withdraw your chips back to your cryptocurrency wallet.

## How Withdrawals Work

Withdrawing converts your HajiPoker chips back into cryptocurrency and sends them to your personal wallet address. The process is straightforward and typically completes within a few hours.

## Step-by-Step Withdrawal Guide

1. Go to your **Wallet** page
2. Click the **Withdraw** button
3. Select which cryptocurrency you'd like to receive your withdrawal in
4. Enter your personal wallet address for that cryptocurrency
5. Enter the amount you'd like to withdraw
6. Review the details carefully — check the address, amount, and network
7. Confirm the withdrawal
8. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, enter your verification code

<Callout type="warn">
Always triple-check your withdrawal address before confirming. Crypto transactions are irreversible — if you enter the wrong address, the funds cannot be recovered.
</Callout>

## Withdrawal Processing Times

Withdrawals are reviewed and processed by our team. Here's what to expect:

| Stage | Estimated Time |
|---|---|
| **Review** | Up to a few hours |
| **Processing** | Depends on the cryptocurrency network |
| **Arrival** | Once the blockchain confirms the transaction |

Most withdrawals are completed within the same day. During busy periods, processing may take slightly longer.

## Important Withdrawal Rules

### Minimum Withdrawal
There is a minimum withdrawal amount to cover network transaction fees. The minimum varies depending on which cryptocurrency you choose.

### Available Balance
You can only withdraw chips that are not currently in play. If you're seated at a poker table, those chips are in use and can't be withdrawn until you leave the table.

### Withdrawal Fees
A small network fee may apply depending on the cryptocurrency you choose. This fee covers the blockchain transaction cost and is clearly shown before you confirm your withdrawal.

## Keeping Your Withdrawals Secure

To protect your funds, we recommend:

- **Enable two-factor authentication** — Withdrawals require your 2FA code when enabled, preventing unauthorized cashouts. See [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication)
- **Use a trusted wallet** — Only withdraw to wallet addresses you personally own and control
- **Verify the network** — Make sure the wallet address matches the correct cryptocurrency network

<Callout type="error">
Never share your HajiPoker password or 2FA codes with anyone. HajiPoker staff will never ask for this information. If someone asks you for these details, it's a scam.
</Callout>

## Withdrawal Troubleshooting

**My withdrawal is taking longer than expected**
Withdrawals go through a review process for security. If your withdrawal has been pending for more than 24 hours, please contact our [support team](/docs/support/getting-help).

**I entered the wrong wallet address**
If the withdrawal hasn't been processed yet, contact support immediately — we may be able to cancel it. Once the crypto has been sent, it cannot be reversed.

**My withdrawal was rejected**
This can happen if there's a security concern on your account. You'll receive a notification explaining the reason. Contact support if you need help resolving it.

**Can I cancel a pending withdrawal?**
If the withdrawal is still in the review stage, you may be able to cancel it by contacting support. Once it's been sent to the blockchain, cancellation is not possible.
