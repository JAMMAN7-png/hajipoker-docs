Every chip movement in your HajiPoker wallet is recorded in your **Transaction History**. This gives you a complete, transparent log of all deposits, withdrawals, wins, losses, and other activity.

## Viewing Your Transaction History

To access your transaction history:

1. Go to your **Wallet** page
2. Scroll down to the **Transaction History** section
3. Browse through your recent transactions

Each transaction entry shows:

- **Date and time** — When the transaction occurred
- **Type** — What kind of transaction it was (deposit, withdrawal, win, etc.)
- **Amount** — How many chips were added or removed
- **Running balance** — Your wallet balance after this transaction

## Transaction Types Explained

Here's what each transaction type means:

| Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Deposit** | Chips in | You added chips to your wallet via crypto or card payment |
| **Withdrawal** | Chips out | You cashed out chips to your crypto wallet |
| **Buy-in** | Chips out | You purchased a seat at a poker table or entered a tournament |
| **Win** | Chips in | You won chips from a poker hand or tournament |
| **Rake** | Chips out | A small service fee was taken from a pot you played in |
| **Bonus** | Chips in | You received promotional or VIP reward chips |
| **Gift** | Both | You sent or received a chip gift from another player |
| **Refund** | Chips in | Chips were returned to you (e.g., a table closed unexpectedly) |

## Understanding Your Transaction Log

### Buy-in and Win Transactions
When you join a poker table, you'll see a **buy-in** transaction for the chips you brought to the table. When you leave a table or win a hand, you'll see **win** transactions for any chips you earned. The difference between your buy-in and your total wins tells you your profit or loss for that session.

### Rake Transactions
A small percentage of each pot is collected as a service fee (called "rake"). This is standard in online poker and is how HajiPoker sustains the platform. Rake transactions appear separately so you can see exactly how much was collected.

<Callout type="info">
If you're a VIP member, you earn **rakeback** — a percentage of the rake paid back to you as bonus chips. The higher your VIP tier, the more rakeback you receive. See [VIP Tiers and Benefits](/docs/vip-program/vip-tiers-and-benefits) for details.
</Callout>

### Bonus and Referral Transactions
Bonus chips from VIP rewards, challenges, or referral bonuses appear as **bonus** type transactions. These are clearly labeled so you can distinguish them from gameplay winnings.

## Transaction Safety

HajiPoker uses a **double-entry bookkeeping** system for all transactions. This means every transaction is recorded twice — once as a debit from one account and once as a credit to another. This accounting method ensures that:

- Your balance is always mathematically accurate
- No chips can appear or disappear without a corresponding record
- Every transaction has a clear paper trail

Additionally, all transactions are protected by **duplicate prevention**. This means if a network glitch or delay causes a transaction request to be sent twice, the system will only process it once. You'll never be charged double for the same action.

## Common Questions

**Q: How far back can I see my transactions?**
Your complete transaction history is available from the day you created your account. Nothing is deleted.

**Q: Can I export my transaction history?**
Currently, you can view your transactions on the website. If you need a copy for your records, contact our [support team](/docs/support/getting-help).

**Q: I see a transaction I don't recognize. What should I do?**
Contact our [support team](/docs/support/getting-help) immediately. Include the date, amount, and type of the suspicious transaction. We'll investigate and help resolve the issue.

**Q: Why do I see multiple small transactions instead of one big one?**
In poker, chips move frequently — every hand you play can generate multiple transactions (buy-in, win, rake). This is normal and gives you a detailed breakdown of your activity.
