Every HajiPoker account comes with a built-in **chip wallet** that is automatically created when you sign up. Your wallet holds the chips you use to play poker, join tournaments, and participate in all activities on the platform.

## What Are Chips?

Chips are the currency used on HajiPoker. Everything on the platform — from buying into a poker table to entering a tournament — uses chips. You can add chips to your wallet by making a deposit and cash them out by requesting a withdrawal.

<Callout type="info">
Your chip balance is displayed in dollar values for easy reading. For example, a balance of 5,000 chips shows as $50.00.
</Callout>

## Viewing Your Balance

You can check your chip balance in several places:

- **Dashboard** — Your balance is displayed on the Balance Card, front and center when you log in
- **Top navigation bar** — Your current balance appears in the header on every page
- **Wallet page** — The dedicated wallet page shows your full balance and recent transactions

## How Chips Move In and Out

There are several ways chips can be added to or removed from your wallet:

| Transaction Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Deposit** | You add chips by sending a crypto payment or using a credit card |
| **Withdrawal** | You cash out chips back to your crypto wallet |
| **Buy-in** | Chips are deducted when you sit down at a poker table |
| **Win** | Chips are added when you win a poker hand or tournament |
| **Rake** | A small percentage taken from each pot as a service fee |
| **Bonus** | Promotional chips added to your account (VIP rewards, etc.) |
| **Gift** | Chips received from or sent to another player |
| **Refund** | Chips returned to you if something went wrong with a transaction |

## Wallet Security

Your wallet is protected by the same security measures as your account:

- **Two-factor authentication** — Add an extra layer of protection by enabling [2FA](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication)
- **Transaction tracking** — Every chip movement is recorded with a timestamp and reason
- **Double-entry bookkeeping** — Every transaction is recorded twice (once as a debit and once as a credit) to ensure your balance is always accurate
- **Duplicate protection** — The system prevents the same transaction from being processed twice, so you'll never be charged double

<Callout type="warn">
Always enable two-factor authentication before making your first deposit. This protects your chips even if someone gains access to your password.
</Callout>

## Minimum and Maximum Limits

When buying into poker tables, each table has its own minimum and maximum buy-in amounts. These are clearly displayed on the table card in the lobby before you join. You can only buy in if you have enough chips in your wallet to meet the table's minimum requirement.

## Common Questions

**Q: Do my chips expire?**
No. Your chip balance stays in your wallet as long as your account is active. There is no expiration.

**Q: Can I have a negative balance?**
No. The system will not allow you to make a purchase or buy-in if you don't have enough chips. Your balance can never go below zero.

**Q: What happens to my chips if I disconnect during a hand?**
If you disconnect during a poker hand, your chips that are already in the pot remain in play. Any chips you haven't bet stay safely in your wallet. If the hand finishes while you're disconnected, any winnings are automatically added to your balance.

**Q: Is my wallet created automatically?**
Yes. As soon as you create a HajiPoker account, a wallet is automatically set up for you with a zero-chip balance, ready for your first deposit.
