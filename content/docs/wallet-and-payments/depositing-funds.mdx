HajiPoker offers two ways to add chips to your wallet: **cryptocurrency payments** and **credit card / bank payments**. This guide covers both methods step by step.

## Option 1: Cryptocurrency Deposits

### How to Deposit with Crypto

1. Go to your **Wallet** page or click the **Deposit** button on your dashboard
2. Select **Crypto** as your payment method
3. Choose the cryptocurrency you want to pay with from the list above
4. Enter the amount you'd like to deposit (in USD value)
5. A unique payment address and QR code will be generated for you
6. Send the exact amount shown from your personal crypto wallet to the provided address
7. Wait for the blockchain network to confirm the transaction — this usually takes a few minutes depending on the cryptocurrency
8. Once confirmed, your chips will be automatically added to your wallet

<Callout type="info">
Stablecoins like USDT, USDC, and BUSD are great choices if you want to avoid price fluctuations during the deposit process. Their value stays pegged to the US dollar.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
Always double-check the wallet address and the network before sending crypto. Sending to the wrong address or wrong network can result in a permanent loss of funds. We cannot recover misdirected crypto payments.
</Callout>

### Crypto Deposit Processing Times

Most crypto deposits are credited within minutes, but times can vary by network:

| Speed | Cryptocurrencies |
|---|---|
| **Fast** (under 5 minutes) | SOL, TRX, TON, XRP, XLM, EOS |
| **Medium** (5–30 minutes) | ETH, BNB, MATIC, ADA, DOT, ATOM, LINK |
| **Slower** (30–60 minutes) | BTC, LTC, BCH, DOGE, ZEC, DASH, XMR |

These times depend on network congestion and can vary.

## Option 2: Credit Card & Bank Payments

If you prefer to pay with a **credit card**, **debit card**, or **bank transfer**, HajiPoker offers a fiat-to-crypto payment widget that makes it easy. You pay in your local currency, and the conversion happens automatically behind the scenes.

### How to Deposit with a Card or Bank

1. Go to your **Wallet** page or click the **Deposit** button
2. Select **Card / Bank** as your payment method
3. Enter the amount you'd like to deposit
4. A secure payment widget will open
5. Enter your card details or select your bank transfer option
6. Complete the payment
7. Once processed, your chips will be added to your wallet

<Callout type="info">
Card and bank payments go through a secure payment partner. Your card details are never stored on HajiPoker's servers.
</Callout>

### Card Payment Availability

Card and bank payment availability may vary depending on your country. The payment widget supports **40+ local payment methods** across **150+ countries**, including:

- Visa and Mastercard credit/debit cards
- Bank transfers
- Local payment methods specific to your region

## After Your Deposit

Once your deposit is processed:

- Your chip balance updates immediately
- A transaction record is created in your [transaction history](/docs/wallet-and-payments/transaction-history)
- You'll see a notification confirming the deposit
- You can immediately use your chips to [join a table](/docs/playing-poker/joining-a-game) or [enter a tournament](/docs/tournaments/joining-a-tournament)

## Deposit Troubleshooting

**My crypto deposit hasn't arrived yet**
Blockchain transactions need network confirmations. Check the transaction on a blockchain explorer using the transaction ID from your wallet. If the transaction shows as confirmed but your chips haven't appeared after 30 minutes, contact our [support team](/docs/support/getting-help).

**My card payment was declined**
This can happen for several reasons — your bank may have flagged the transaction, or the payment method might not be available in your region. Try a different card, use a bank transfer, or switch to a crypto deposit.

**I sent crypto to the wrong address**
Unfortunately, crypto transactions are irreversible. Always triple-check the address before sending. If you sent to the wrong address, the funds cannot be recovered.
