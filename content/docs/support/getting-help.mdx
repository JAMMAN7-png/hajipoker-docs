Need help? HajiPoker offers several ways to get support, whether you have a question, encountered an issue, or need assistance with your account.

## Live Chat Support

The fastest way to get help is through our **live chat** widget. You'll find a chat bubble in the **bottom-right corner** of every page on HajiPoker.

### How to Use Live Chat

1. Look for the **chat icon** in the bottom-right corner of the page
2. Click on it to open the chat window
3. Type your question or describe your issue
4. A support team member will respond as quickly as possible

Live chat is perfect for:
- Quick questions about features
- Help with deposits or withdrawals
- Account issues
- Gameplay questions
- Reporting problems

<Callout type="info">
Live chat support is available around the clock. Our team aims to respond to your messages as quickly as possible.
</Callout>

## What to Include in Your Support Request

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

### For Account Issues
- Your **username** or **email address**
- A description of the problem
- Any error messages you're seeing
- What steps you've already tried

### For Payment Issues
- Your **username**
- The **transaction type** (deposit, withdrawal)
- The **amount** and **cryptocurrency** involved
- The **date and time** of the transaction
- Any **transaction IDs** from your external wallet

### For Gameplay Issues
- The **game type** and **table** you were playing at
- The **hand number** if applicable
- What happened versus what you expected
- The **date and time** of the incident

### For Fairness Concerns
- The **hand number** or hand details
- Your [verification](/docs/provably-fair/verifying-your-hands) results if you've checked
- Any screenshots or details that might help

## Common Issues We Can Help With

| Issue | What We Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Can't log in** | Help reset your password or troubleshoot login issues |
| **Deposit not arriving** | Check blockchain confirmations and investigate delays |
| **Withdrawal pending** | Provide status updates and estimated processing times |
| **Account verification** | Walk you through the verification process |
| **Suspicious activity** | Investigate and secure your account |
| **Game rules questions** | Explain rules, hand rankings, and gameplay mechanics |
| **VIP program questions** | Clarify tier requirements, points, and rakeback |
| **Technical problems** | Help troubleshoot bugs or display issues |

## Responsible Gaming

If you're concerned about your gaming habits or spending:

- Take a break from playing
- Set personal limits on your deposits and playing time
- Contact our support team if you need help managing your gaming activity
- We take responsible gaming seriously and will work with you to find solutions

<Callout type="warn">
If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately through live chat. We can temporarily freeze your account to prevent unauthorized access while we investigate.
</Callout>

## Tips for a Smooth Support Experience

1. **Be specific** — The more details you provide, the faster we can help
2. **Include screenshots** — If you're seeing an error or unexpected behavior, a screenshot helps enormously
3. **Check the FAQ first** — Your question might already have an answer in our [FAQ](/docs/support/faq)
4. **Be patient** — During busy periods, response times may be slightly longer than usual
5. **One issue per chat** — If you have multiple issues, addressing them one at a time leads to better results
