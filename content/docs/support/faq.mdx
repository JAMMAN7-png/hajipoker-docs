## Account & Sign-Up

### How do I create an account?
Click the **Sign Up** button on the website and fill in your email, username, and password. You can also sign up with Google, Discord, or Telegram for faster registration. See our [full sign-up guide](/docs/authentication/creating-your-account).

### Can I change my username?
You can update your username from the **Settings** page. Your new username will be visible at all poker tables.

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Click the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page, enter your email address, and follow the instructions in the reset email. See [Resetting Your Password](/docs/authentication/resetting-your-password) for more details.

### Is two-factor authentication required?
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is optional but **strongly recommended**. It adds an extra layer of security to protect your chips and personal information. See [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication).

### Can I sign in with Google, Discord, or Telegram?
Yes! HajiPoker supports social login with Google, Discord, and Telegram. See [Social Login](/docs/authentication/social-login).

## Wallet & Payments

### How do I deposit chips?
Go to your Wallet page and choose either **Crypto** or **Card/Bank** payment. We support 25 cryptocurrencies and credit/debit card payments. See [Depositing Funds](/docs/wallet-and-payments/depositing-funds).

### Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?
We accept 25 cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Tether (USDT), USD Coin (USDC), Solana (SOL), Litecoin (LTC), Dogecoin (DOGE), Ripple (XRP), Cardano (ADA), Polkadot (DOT), Toncoin (TON), TRON (TRX), and many more. See the [full list](/docs/wallet-and-payments/depositing-funds).

### How long do deposits take?
Crypto deposits depend on the blockchain network — some arrive in under 5 minutes (SOL, TRX, TON), while others may take up to an hour (BTC). Card payments are usually processed within minutes.

### How do I withdraw my chips?
Go to your Wallet page, click **Withdraw**, choose your cryptocurrency, enter your wallet address, and confirm the amount. See [Withdrawing Funds](/docs/wallet-and-payments/withdrawing-funds).

### Is there a minimum deposit or withdrawal amount?
Minimum amounts may apply depending on the cryptocurrency and network fees involved. The specific minimums are shown in the deposit/withdrawal interface.

### Can I use a credit card to deposit?
Yes! HajiPoker accepts credit and debit cards through a secure payment widget that supports Visa, Mastercard, and 40+ local payment methods across 150+ countries.

### Are my funds safe?
Yes. Every transaction is recorded with double-entry bookkeeping, and duplicate transactions are prevented automatically. We strongly recommend enabling [2FA](/docs/authentication/two-factor-authentication) for additional account protection.

## Playing Poker

### What poker games are available?
HajiPoker offers three game types:
- **Texas Hold'em** — 2 hole cards (the classic)
- **Omaha 4-Card** — 4 hole cards (more action)
- **Omaha 5-Card** — 5 hole cards (maximum action)

See [Game Types](/docs/playing-poker/game-types) for full details.

### How many players can sit at a table?
Tables support between **2 and 9 players**. You can find tables of various sizes in the [lobby](/docs/playing-poker/finding-a-table).

### What are the default blinds?
The default blind level is **25/50** (small blind 25 chips, big blind 50 chips), but tables are available at various stake levels.

### Can I create a private table for friends?
Yes! You can create a password-protected table with custom settings. Only players who know the password can join. See [Creating a Private Table](/docs/playing-poker/creating-a-private-table).

### What happens if I disconnect during a hand?
Your chips in the pot stay in play. Unbet chips remain safe in your stack. If you don't reconnect in time, your hand may be folded automatically.

### What is heads-up poker?
Heads-up is one-on-one poker with just 2 players at the table. See [Heads-Up Poker](/docs/playing-poker/heads-up).

## Tournaments

### What type of tournaments do you offer?
HajiPoker offers **Sit & Go tournaments** that start as soon as enough players register (minimum 2 players). See [How Tournaments Work](/docs/tournaments/how-tournaments-work).

### How many chips do I start with in a tournament?
All players start with **10,000 chips**, regardless of the buy-in amount.

### How often do blinds increase?
Blinds increase every **5 minutes** across 10 levels, from 10/20 up to 500/1,000. See the [full blind schedule](/docs/tournaments/how-tournaments-work).

### How are prizes distributed?
- **7+ players**: 1st gets 50%, 2nd gets 30%, 3rd gets 20%
- **2–6 players**: 1st gets 65%, 2nd gets 35%

See [Prizes and Payouts](/docs/tournaments/prizes-and-payouts).

### Can I re-enter a tournament after being eliminated?
No. Once you lose all your chips in a Sit & Go tournament, you're eliminated. There are no rebuys or re-entries.

## Provably Fair

### Is HajiPoker fair?
Yes! HajiPoker uses a **provably fair** system that lets you mathematically verify that every hand is dealt randomly. See [What Is Provably Fair](/docs/provably-fair/what-is-provably-fair).

### How do I verify a hand?
Go to your hand history, click on any completed hand, and use the built-in verification tool. It checks the mathematical seed against the cards dealt. See [Verifying Your Hands](/docs/provably-fair/verifying-your-hands).

### Can HajiPoker manipulate the cards?
No. The provably fair system locks in the card order before the hand starts using an encrypted seed. This seed cannot be changed after it's created, making manipulation impossible.

## VIP Program

### How does the VIP program work?
Play poker to earn VIP points. As you accumulate points, you climb through 5 tiers (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond) with increasing rakeback rewards from 5% to 30%. See [VIP Tiers and Benefits](/docs/vip-program/vip-tiers-and-benefits).

### What is rakeback?
Rakeback is a percentage of the rake (service fee) returned to you as bonus chips. Your rakeback percentage depends on your VIP tier — from 5% at Bronze to 30% at Diamond.

### How many points do I need for each tier?
- **Bronze**: 0 points (starting tier, 5% rakeback)
- **Silver**: 1,000 points (10% rakeback)
- **Gold**: 5,000 points (15% rakeback)
- **Platinum**: 15,000 points (20% rakeback)
- **Diamond**: 50,000 points (30% rakeback)

### Are there VIP challenges?
Yes! Complete challenges like playing 100 hands (100 chip reward), winning 10 tournaments (500 chip reward), referring 5 friends (300 chip reward), reaching Silver (200 chip reward), or reaching Gold (500 chip reward). See [Challenges and Rewards](/docs/vip-program/challenges-and-rewards).

## Referrals

### How does the referral program work?
Share your unique referral code with friends. When they sign up and start playing, **you get 500 chips** and **they get 200 chips**. See [Invite Friends](/docs/referrals/invite-friends).

### Is there a limit on referrals?
No! You can refer as many friends as you like and earn 500 chips for each one.

### Can I apply a referral code after creating my account?
No. The referral code must be used during registration. It can't be applied retroactively.

## Technical Issues

### The website isn't loading properly. What should I do?
Try these steps:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Try a different browser
3. Check your internet connection
4. Disable any browser extensions that might interfere
5. If the problem persists, contact [support](/docs/support/getting-help)

### I'm not receiving emails from HajiPoker.
Check your spam/junk folder first. If emails are there, mark them as "not spam." If you still aren't receiving emails, contact [support](/docs/support/getting-help) to verify your email address.
